## Short description (≤ 300 chars)
Pip turns one sentence about your business into a complete brand: direction, logo, colours
and fonts, a real website, print-ready posters and social graphics, plus prompts for Midjourney,
Flux, Kling and Veo — all from one brand file, so everything matches.

## Long description
Most design skills do one thing. Pip runs the whole studio:

- **Brand** — positioning, three directions to pick from, moodboard, colours, fonts, brand book.
- **Logo** — four SVG directions: wordmark, monogram, lockup, badge.
- **Website** — built by a full web engine with real self-hosted fonts and checks at four screen sizes.
- **Print & social** — A2–A6 and 50×70 posters, flyers, business cards, Instagram posts and stories:
  print-ready PDF with bleed + PNG.
- **Photo** — shoot style guide and prompts for Midjourney, Flux and SDXL.
- **Video** — script, storyboard and prompts for Kling, Runway, Luma and Veo.
- **Code** — 9 agents (review, security, e2e tests, TDD) and 15 developer commands.

Everything comes from one `brand.json`: change a colour once and the site, posters and prompts
update together. Decisions are grounded in a built-in design library (89 styles, 192 palettes,
74 font pairings, 120 UX rules) and every palette is contrast-checked against WCAG.

**28 commands** — start with `/heypip:start`; `/heypip:help` lists the rest.
Works in Claude Code (full plugin) and on claude.ai (skill). Beginner guide included.
