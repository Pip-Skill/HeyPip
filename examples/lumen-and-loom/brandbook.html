Imagery

Every photo and generated image follows these.

  • light: hard low late-afternoon Lisbon sun raking through wooden window shutters, crisp stripes of shadow on limewashed walls
  • grade: matte sun-bleached film grade, warm whites, lifted blacks, fine 35mm grain, muted saturation, indigo kept deep
  • lens: 35mm and 50mm, f/2.8 to f/4, natural perspective, eye-level or slightly above
  • framing: asymmetric, subject in one third, large clean wall or floor area for type, crops through the body rather than full-length catalogue poses
  • subjects: seamstresses' hands at work, raw and washed linen, finished garments worn by real people aged 30 to 60, Lisbon limestone, worn wooden tables, calçada pavement
  • palette_words: limewash white, raw flax, charcoal, deep indigo

Avoid

  • seamless studio backdrop
  • flat diffused light
  • heavy retouching
  • steamed glossy fabric
  • posed smiles
  • neon
  • teal-orange grade
  • azulejo tile cliché
  • lens flare

Film and motion pictures

Pace, camera and sound for video.

  • pace: slow, 3 to 5 second shots
  • camera: locked-off tripod or slow lateral slide, no handheld shake, no drones
  • cuts: straight cuts on the moment a needle or scissors moves; match cut from shutter shadow to striped linen
  • sound: room tone, scissors on wood, treadle sewing machine, distant tram bell
  • type_in_motion: Cormorant Garamond, opacity fades only, 600ms
