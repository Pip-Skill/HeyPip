Positioning

Independent neighbourhood coffee roastery-café (Kiezrösterei), Berlin-Neukölln

What makes it different

Roasts every Wednesday in the guest room itself — 14 seats, neighbours see, smell and hear their coffee being made and take the beans home the same week.

neighbourlyexactwarm-smoky

Is

  • a working room with a log book
  • exact numbers (batch, °C, date)
  • bilingual the way neighbours talk
  • kraft, ink, ember
  • Wednesday as a ritual

Is not

  • a latte-art Instagram set
  • vague 'artisan passion' talk
  • tourist-English menu
  • marble, gold, pastel
  • 'experience', 'journey'

Voice

plain, neighbourly, precise

Do

  • numbers instead of adjectives (batch, °C, minutes, date)
  • German 'du/ihr' Kiez register; Russian «вы», warm not stiff
  • short declaratives
  • same structure in DE and RU

Don't

  • artisan / passion / Erlebnis / experience / крафтовый / атмосферный
  • English as a third language
  • invented reviews, stats or awards
  • exclamation marks

Imagery

Every photo and generated image follows these.

  • light: warm tungsten inside mixed with grey Berlin daylight from the street window
  • grade: warm browns, matte, lifted blacks, visible film grain, no teal-orange
  • lens: 35mm documentary, 50mm for details, f/2–f/2.8
  • framing: close on hands, drum, cooling tray, steam; neighbours candid, not posed
  • subjects: the roaster in the guest room, beans cooling, kraft bags with handwritten batch numbers, regulars at the 14 seats
  • palette_words: kraft, roast brown, ink, ember

Avoid

  • marble flat-lays
  • latte-art hero shots
  • stock baristas
  • white seamless
  • neon

Film and motion pictures

Pace, camera and sound for video.

  • pace: slow, 3–5s shots, Wednesday morning in real time
  • camera: handheld but calm, or locked-off on the drum; no whip pans
  • cuts: straight cuts on sound (first crack, tray opening)
  • sound: drum rumble, first crack, cups, street through the door; no music bed or sparse
  • type_in_motion: Alegreya headlines + Plex Mono data, fades only, 520ms
