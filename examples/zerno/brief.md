# Brief — Зерно / Zerno

**Assumptions (decided without asking — correct me):**
- Name is written «Зерно» in Russian copy and **Zerno** in German/Latin contexts (logo shows both).
- Price level: mid, neighbourhood specialty — not a premium "third-wave temple".
- No logo, colours or stack exist yet; site is a static one-pager, no shop/checkout.
- Address, opening hours, prices, roast-day time slot and bean origins are unknown → marked `[ADRESSE]`, `[ÖFFNUNGSZEITEN]`, `[PREIS]`, `[UHRZEIT]`, `[HERKUNFT]`.
- German is the primary language (default page, `lang="de"`); Russian is a full second version, not a summary. Fonts must cover Latin Extended (ä ö ü ß) and Cyrillic.

**What it is:** a small coffee roastery-café in Berlin-Neukölln, 14 seats, that roasts its beans every Wednesday right in the room; coffee to drink in and beans to take home, at neighbourhood prices.
**Audience:** people who live within a few blocks (Reuterkiez / Schillerkiez type neighbours, German- and Russian-speaking), who today go to a Späti, a chain, or one of the many "laptop cafés". They care about knowing who made their coffee and not being treated as tourists; they're put off by Instagram-latte pretension and by prices aimed at visitors.
**The job of this work:** a brand and a one-page site that make neighbours (1) come in, (2) come on Wednesday to watch/smell the roast, (3) buy beans to take home.
**Must keep:** nothing existing. Bilingual DE/RU.
**Constraints:** two languages with different scripts; small business → simple, cheap to maintain, works on phones; WCAG AA.
**Success looks like:** people mention "Mittwoch" / "среда" — the roast day becomes the thing the neighbourhood knows them for.
